Small steps, big patterns

A half step is one neighboring piano key, including black keys. A whole step is two. Major scales follow whole–whole–half–whole–whole–whole–half.

Same key, two names

C♯ and D♭ are enharmonic names for the same pitch in equal temperament. An octave is 12 half steps; its frequency doubles.

Build a chord

A triad uses three notes. Major: root + 4 + 7 half steps. Minor: root + 3 + 7. Change just the middle note and listen.
